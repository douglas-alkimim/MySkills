# Planning Audit

Apply Essential depth to Compact, Complete depth to Standard, and Detailed depth to Extensive. Report concisely when no issue exists; report each finding with evidence.

## Essential — every plan

- [ ] Outcome and deliverables are explicit.
- [ ] Exact identifiers, paths, values, and compatibility constraints that affect execution are preserved.
- [ ] Project evidence was inspected before asking discoverable questions.
- [ ] Planning depth matches actual risk, uncertainty, dependencies, deliverables, external effects, reversibility, systems, and coordination.
- [ ] Every wave and item satisfies its complexity-appropriate schema.
- [ ] Plan language is selected, tagged, and consistent.
- [ ] Strict wave sequencing and same-wave independence are preserved.
- [ ] Final acceptance proves the requested outcome.
- [ ] The plan uses the fewest useful work units: splitting creates independence, better verification, or reduced risk.

## Complete — Standard in addition

- [ ] Scope and exclusions are clear when boundaries matter.
- [ ] Facts, decisions, assumptions, and unknowns are separated when present.
- [ ] Material unknowns have a decision, discovery item, prototype, or blocker.
- [ ] Requirements, contracts, dependencies, integration, and traceability are complete.
- [ ] Applicable domain risks, governance, and authorization boundaries are explicit.

## Detailed — Extensive in addition

- [ ] Stakeholders, authority, external effects, environments, and operational controls are detailed.
- [ ] Significant risks have prevention, detection, response ownership, and evidence.
- [ ] Difficult rollback and recovery paths have triggers, procedures, preserved state, and verification.
- [ ] Multi-agent or multi-discipline coordination justifies its overhead and includes isolation and integration controls.

## Conditional checks

- [ ] Domain modules were loaded only when relevant.
- [ ] Risk analysis is present when consequences warrant it.
- [ ] Recovery analysis is present when reversibility or failure impact warrants it.
