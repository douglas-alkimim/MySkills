# Coverage Audit

Coverage is mandatory; its representation scales with complexity.

## Compact — direct verification

- [ ] Every requested outcome or material requirement maps directly to an item, output, observable criterion, and final check.
- [ ] Every item supports an outcome, requirement, risk, integration, or verification need.
- [ ] The union of item objectives covers each wave objective.
- [ ] Item and integration criteria imply each wave criterion and final acceptance.

A concise list such as `R001 → I001 → output → check` is sufficient. Do not require an empty or redundant matrix.

## Standard — traceability matrix

- [ ] Every material requirement has a stable ID.
- [ ] Every requirement maps to responsible items, outputs, and measurable evidence.
- [ ] Cross-cutting contracts appear in all affected waves and prompts.
- [ ] No changed requirement leaves a stale prompt or criterion.
- [ ] Out-of-scope work is not smuggled into an item.

## Extensive — full traceability

- [ ] Sources, decisions, risks, stakeholders, contracts, items, outputs, verification, and final impact are connected where applicable.
- [ ] Traceability is audited in both directions for orphan requirements and orphan work.
- [ ] Recovery, operational, regulatory, and approval evidence is mapped to its governing requirement or risk.
