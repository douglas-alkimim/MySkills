# Requirement Traceability

Assign stable requirement IDs such as R001. Preserve the user’s wording where it carries contractual meaning.

Traceability must connect:

- requirement;
- source or rationale;
- responsible item or items;
- output;
- verification criterion and evidence;
- final acceptance impact.

Compact may express this as direct mappings rather than a matrix. Standard normally uses a traceability matrix; Extensive includes full sources, decisions, risks, contracts, approvals, and operational impacts where applicable.

Audit in both directions. Every material requirement needs an owner and proof. Every item must serve a requirement, risk, integration need, or verification need. Resolve orphan requirements before ready status. Remove or justify orphan work rather than hiding it as “cleanup.”

When a requirement changes, identify affected contracts, waves, items, prompts, tests, and relations. Regenerate every affected prompt and update the revision record.
