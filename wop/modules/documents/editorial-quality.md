# Editorial Quality

Define the audience’s decisions, prior knowledge, and reading context. Give each section one job and make the argument or workflow explicit.

Audit:

- factual claims against cited or authoritative sources;
- reasoning for unsupported leaps or contradictions;
- mathematical symbols, units, assumptions, and reproducibility;
- methods against the stated research or business question;
- terminology, abbreviations, tone, and narrative continuity;
- tables and figures for truthful labels, units, and source notes;
- citations for coverage, correctness, and reference-list agreement;
- grammar and style without flattening the intended voice.

Acceptance requires both content correctness and rendered usability. Do not mark a document accepted from source text alone when pagination, formulas, figures, or navigation matter.
