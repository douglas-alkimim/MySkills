# Documents Planning Module

Apply to authored documents, reports, manuscripts, spreadsheets, presentations, and rendered deliverables.

Plan for:

- audience, purpose, voice, language, and required format;
- factual, logical, mathematical, and methodological correctness;
- source quality, citation coverage, and reference consistency;
- structure, terminology, cross-references, and editorial consistency;
- accessibility and organization-specific requirements;
- source-file validation plus rendered-output inspection;
- revision, approval, and delivery format.

Natural human-sounding prose is an outcome, not permission to introduce unsupported claims. Separate content review from layout review and final acceptance.

Load [editorial-quality.md](editorial-quality.md) plus only the relevant format modules: [word.md](word.md), [latex.md](latex.md), [pdf.md](pdf.md), [spreadsheets.md](spreadsheets.md), or [presentations.md](presentations.md). When multiple formats derive from one source, declare the authoritative source and synchronization method.
