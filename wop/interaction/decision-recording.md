# Decision Recording

Record durable decisions in the plan, not only in conversation.

For each material decision capture:

- decision ID or requirement link;
- chosen option;
- rationale and trade-off;
- decision owner;
- timestamp when relevant;
- affected contracts, waves, or criteria;
- alternatives rejected;
- revisit trigger, if any.

Confirmed facts require source or inspection evidence. Assumptions require impact and validation plan. Unknowns require an owner, discovery path, or explicit blocker. Never relabel an assumption as a fact because it is convenient.

When a decision changes, update the authoritative section, traceability, affected prompts, and revision history. Remove stale text rather than leaving contradictory guidance.
