# Question Strategy

## Classify missing information

- Discoverable fact: inspect it.
- User preference or authority decision: ask it.
- Expert recommendation: offer a recommendation with trade-offs and invite correction.
- Empirical uncertainty: create a prototype, measurement, or discovery item.
- External dependency: record owner, expected evidence, and blocking condition.

## Question frontier

Represent decision B as blocked when it depends on answer A. Ask A first. Recompute the frontier after every answer. Avoid hypothetical branches whose prerequisite choice has not been made.

A useful question states why the answer matters, offers mutually distinct choices when appropriate, and makes the effect on scope or acceptance visible. Avoid broad “anything else?” prompts until the known decision graph is exhausted.

Do not repeat answered questions. If new evidence reopens a decision, say what changed and which prior conclusion is affected.
