# Context Efficiency

Spend context on decisions and constraints that affect execution.

- Replace repeated prose with stable IDs and authoritative contracts.
- Load only the operation and domain modules needed now.
- Prefer tables for repeated mappings such as traceability and dependencies.
- Keep examples outside normative rules unless they clarify a fragile format.
- Remove historical sediment from current instructions; retain it in revision history.
- Regenerate prompts rather than appending corrections.
- Omit irrelevant Compact sections and optional fields instead of recording empty-state filler.
- Do not fragment cohesive work into extra waves, items, or agents unless the split improves independence, verification, or risk.

Self-containment and efficiency are compatible: include the smallest complete set of relevant facts, not every fact in the project.
