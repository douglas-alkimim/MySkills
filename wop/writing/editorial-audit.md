# Editorial Audit

Before ready status, verify:

- terminology and identifiers are consistent;
- every instruction has an actor or clear subject;
- pronouns do not hide ownership;
- every “must” is testable or governance-critical;
- no critical behavior depends on “as discussed,” prior chat, or unstated context;
- facts, assumptions, decisions, and unknowns are not blended;
- shared contracts have one authoritative definition;
- prompts contain no stale version, criterion, path, or predecessor;
- headings and lists make scanning possible;
- every sentence passes relevance and no-op tests;
- brevity has not removed necessary execution context.

Resolve contradictions in favor of the authoritative plan, then regenerate derivative prompts.
