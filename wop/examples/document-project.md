# Example: Scientific Document

Request: produce a LaTeX manuscript and submission PDF from provided analysis.

## Module routing

Load the document module, editorial quality, LaTeX, and PDF modules. Do not load Word, spreadsheets, or presentations unless they are deliverables.

## Wave shape

W001 validates sources, claims, methods, calculations, target-journal constraints, and bibliography inputs. A methodological unknown becomes a discovery or author decision before drafting.

W002 may parallelize sections only after terminology, outline, notation, evidence boundaries, and citation contract are frozen. Section owners must not redefine shared claims or notation.

W003 integrates prose, cross-references, figures, tables, and bibliography.

W004 compiles, reviews logs, verifies citations/references, checks mathematical and factual consistency, and inspects every rendered PDF page.

Acceptance includes a reproducible build, no unresolved references, compliant structure, verified claims, readable equations and figures, and page-level visual inspection. Successful compilation alone is insufficient.
